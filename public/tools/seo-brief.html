What Is Search Intent?

Search intent is the underlying purpose behind a search query. Understanding intent helps SEO professionals and content creators create pages that better match what searchers are looking for.

Types of Search Intent

Informational Intent

The searcher is looking for information, an explanation, answer, guide, or educational resource.

Commercial Intent

The searcher is researching products, services, brands, or solutions before making a decision.

Navigational Intent

The searcher is trying to find a particular website, brand, page, or online destination.

Transactional Intent

The searcher is ready to take an action such as purchasing, signing up, downloading, or booking.

Why Search Intent Matters for SEO

Matching content with search intent can help you choose the right page type, content format, keywords, headings, calls to action, and conversion strategy for a target query.
