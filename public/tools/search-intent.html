Search intent explained

What is search intent?

Search intent is the reason behind a user's search query. A person may be looking for information, researching products or services, finding a specific website, or preparing to make a purchase.

Identifying intent helps SEO professionals and content creators understand what type of page may best satisfy a search query. The SeachLoom AI Search Intent Analyzer is designed to help organize queries around common intent categories.

Types of search intent

Informational Learn Commercial Research Navigational Find Transactional Act

Informational intent

Informational searches are generally used when someone wants to learn about a topic, answer a question, or understand how something works.

Commercial intent

Commercial searches typically indicate that a user is researching options before making a decision, such as comparing products, services, or solutions.

Navigational intent

Navigational searches are generally intended to reach a particular website, brand, page, or online destination.

Transactional intent

Transactional searches indicate that a user may be ready to take an action such as purchasing, signing up, downloading, booking, or completing another conversion activity.
