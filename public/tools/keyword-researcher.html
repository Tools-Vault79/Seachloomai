What Is Keyword Research?

Keyword research is the process of finding and evaluating the search terms people use in search engines. It can help website owners, marketers and content creators understand what their audience is looking for and identify topics worth covering.

A useful keyword should be relevant to your website and audience. It should also match the purpose of the page you are creating. Good keyword research therefore considers more than a single number or metric.

  3. Identify relevant opportunities. Look for keywords that closely match your audience, topic and content goals.
  4. Consider search intent. Determine what the searcher is trying to accomplish before selecting a keyword for a page.
  5. Use the ideas in your SEO strategy. Select relevant terms for website pages, articles and content planning.

Choose Keywords Based on Search Intent

A keyword becomes more useful when it matches what the searcher is actually trying to accomplish. Understanding search intent can help you decide whether a keyword belongs on an informational article, commercial landing page, product page or another type of content.

Informational Keywords

Informational searches are usually performed when someone wants to learn, understand or solve a problem. These keywords can be useful for educational articles, guides and tutorials.

Commercial Keywords

Commercial searches often indicate that someone is researching products, services or solutions before making a decision.

Transactional Keywords

Transactional searches can indicate that someone is ready to take an action such as buying, signing up or using a service.

What Makes a Good SEO Keyword?

A good SEO keyword should be relevant to the subject of your website and useful to the people you want to reach. Consider search intent, topic relevance, content quality and whether your page can provide a better and more useful answer than competing results.

Keyword research should be treated as a starting point for SEO decision-making rather than a guarantee of rankings or traffic.
